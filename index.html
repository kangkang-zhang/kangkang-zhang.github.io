I'm Kangkang Zhang, an Assistant Professor in Accountancy at Gies College of Business, the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Before joining Gies in 2025, I received a Ph.D. in Accounting from the University of Connecticut. My research interests are primarily in financial accounting, with specific interests in securities regulation, the real effects of regulatory scrutiny, mandatory and voluntary disclosure, and public finance.

Research Interests

  • Institutional investor governance
  • SEC enforcement and regulatory quality
  • Corporate information environments
